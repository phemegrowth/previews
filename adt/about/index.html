About ADT

We look after property as if it were ours

Founded 2023 Limassol

Based in Limassol. Trusted across Cyprus.

ADT started in Limassol in 2023. Our clients kept asking us the same thing: who can we trust to look after our property properly? Today we answer that ourselves, with property management, building management and the services around them.

We work with private owners in Cyprus and abroad, companies, owners with growing portfolios, residential buildings and developers, all over the island. What they share is simple: they want their property professionally managed.

We combine personal service, experienced local professionals, proactive management and our own owner platform. Our team speaks English, Greek and Hebrew, and our office is in Germasogeia, Limassol.
