Property management

Your property in Cyprus. Managed to a different standard.

Whether you own one apartment or an entire portfolio, and whether you're based in Cyprus or abroad, ADT takes care of the details that make property ownership effortless. Responsible, long-term tenants

We support careful tenant selection and look after the relationship from day one, putting responsible long-term occupancy and the condition of your property first.

Rent monitoring and follow-up

We track every payment and follow up on time, so the rent arrives when it should and nothing is left to chase.
