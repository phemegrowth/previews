Building management

A building that runs well, and accounts that add up

For owners and committees of residential buildings in Limassol who want the common areas cared for and the common expenses clear.

Common areas

Entrances, stairs, lifts, gardens and pools: cleaned, maintained and checked on a regular schedule.

Common expenses

A yearly budget, fair collection from every owner, and clear accounts that anyone in the building can read.

Contractors and repairs

We find, brief and check the contractors, from small repairs to larger works, and keep the building’s records up to date.

A manager you can reach

A dedicated building manager in Limassol who knows your building, answers the phone and speaks Greek and English.

How we start

Four steps to a building that runs well

  1. 01

    We meet the committee

    We listen to what works in the building today and what doesn’t.

  2. 02

    We walk the building

    Common areas, equipment, contracts and accounts.

  3. 03

  4. 04
