The villa

Quiet, bright, and all yours

Dion Villa sits in the hills of Volimes, in the north of Zakynthos, close to Navagio and the island's most famous coastline. Stone walls outside, white marble inside, and big windows facing the sea.

There is one king bedroom (it can become two singles), a double sofa bed in the living room, two bathrooms and a full kitchen. You have the pool, the jacuzzi and the terrace to yourselves.

Your host, Dionusia, lives nearby and helps with airport transfers, car hire and day trips.

Private pool and terrace
