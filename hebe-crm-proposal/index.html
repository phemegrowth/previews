Proposal · A custom CRM for Hebe Medicare

We would like to build Hebe Medicare its own CRM.

One online system, made only for you, where your team manages trainings and bookings, doctors and clinics, product orders, invoices and client statements. It replaces the CRM you use today.

The offer in short

What we are offering, in plain words.

What it is

A web system (a CRM) built from zero around how Hebe works. It opens in the browser on a computer or phone. Each person on your team has their own login.

What it replaces

Your current CRM, and the sheets, notes and emails used to fill its gaps. Your existing contacts and enquiries are moved across, so nothing is lost.

Who does what

Pheme Growth designs it, builds it, sets it up and looks after it. Your team tells us how you work and uses it. If you need a change later, you ask us.

What your team will be able to do

  1. Trainings: see every training date with seats taken, seats left and a waiting list.
  2. Bookings: see who is coming to each training, who has paid, and send reminders.
  3. Doctors and clinics: one page per client with their trainings, orders, invoices and balance.
  4. Products: track orders, stock, lot numbers and expiry dates.
  5. Invoices and statements: make an invoice from a booking or order, and send a client their statement in one click.
  6. Ads: see which ads bring doctors who actually book and pay.

Each of these six parts is shown below as a draft screen.

4 · Products, orders and stock

Know what you have, what's running low, and where every lot went.

  • Stock per product, with lot numbers and expiry dates, updated with every order and delivery.
  • An alert before a product runs out, and before a lot gets close to expiry.
  • Every lot is tied to the clinics that received it. If a supplier ever recalls a batch, you have the list in one click.

Injectables are medical devices. Knowing exactly which clinic got which lot protects Hebe and the doctors.

5 · Invoices and statements

Invoice from the booking or the order. Statement in one click.

  • Overdue invoices are flagged, with a polite reminder ready to send.
  • Everything exports for your accountant, so nothing is typed twice.
