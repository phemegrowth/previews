How it works

From an idea to your living room

1.

Tell us

Fill in the form or send a WhatsApp with a photo, a sketch or the space you have.

2.

We meet and measure

We come to your home, take measurements and talk about wood, colour and budget.

3.

3D design

You see your piece in 3D before anything is cut, and change it until it is right.

4.

Made by hand

We build it in our workshop in Nicosia, from solid wood and quality boards.

5.

Delivered and fitted

We deliver and install it ourselves, and leave the room clean.

The workshop

Wood you can feel

We choose the wood with you: warm oak, dark walnut, light ash or pine for a natural look. Then we cut, join and finish every piece by hand.
