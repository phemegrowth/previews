About

Made by hand, made to last

A.C Furniture is a small workshop in Nicosia. We design and build custom furniture for homes across Cyprus, one piece at a time.

Why custom

Because your home is not standard

  • 01It fits the space exactly, corner to corner, floor to ceiling.
  • 02You choose the wood, the colour, the handles and every detail.
  • 03You see it in 3D before we build it.
  • 04Solid materials and real joints, not furniture you replace in five years.

How it works

Five steps, no surprises

1.

Tell us

Fill in the form or send a WhatsApp with a photo, a sketch or the space you have.

2.

We meet and measure

We come to your home, take measurements and talk about wood, colour and budget.

3.

3D design

You see your piece in 3D before anything is cut, and change it until it is right.

4.

Made by hand

We build it in our workshop in Nicosia, from solid wood and quality boards.

5.

Delivered and fitted

We deliver and install it ourselves, and leave the room clean.
