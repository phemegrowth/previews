Services

One cook. Three ways to hire him.

Whether it is a dinner for four, a menu for a hotel or a wedding for a hundred and fifty, the method is the same: listen first, write the menu, then cook it properly.

01

Private chef

A restaurant-level dinner in your own dining room, on your terrace or on your boat. I plan the menu with you, shop for the produce, cook, serve and leave the kitchen clean.

Best for · 2 to 20 guests · villas, apartments, yachts

Ask about this

  • Tasting menus of 4 to 7 courses
  • Family-style dinners and long tables
  • Weekly or holiday chef for villas
  • Wine and drinks pairing on request
  • Allergies and diets handled in the plan
02

Menu consulting

For restaurants and hotels that want a menu that sells and a kitchen that can deliver it every night. I work with owners and head chefs, on site, with the real numbers.

Best for · hotels, restaurants, beach clubs · openings and relaunches

Ask about this

  • Menu engineering and seasonal rewrites
  • Recipe costing and portion control
  • Breakfast, à la carte and banqueting menus
  • Training the brigade before an opening
  • Supplier sourcing in Cyprus and Greece
03

Catering

Food for the day that matters, cooked fresh on site wherever possible. I design the menu, bring the team and the kit, and handle service from the first canapé to the last coffee.

Best for · 20 to 200 guests · venues, gardens, beaches

Ask about this

  • Canapé receptions and cocktail menus
  • Long-table dinners and grilled menus
  • Weddings, baptisms and anniversaries
  • Company events and product launches
  • Staff, tableware and set-up on request
Next step

Tell me the date, the guests and the mood. I will write the menu.
