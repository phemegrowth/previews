About

A cook first. Then everything else.

Giorgos grew up between the tables of a family taverna, where the day began with bread and ended with the last pan scrubbed. He learned to cook before he learned to call it a career.

Twenty years later he has run the pass in an Athens tasting-menu kitchen, opened a boutique hotel on the Aegean and led eighteen cooks at a resort in Cyprus. The thread is the same: honest produce, cooked with fire and timing, served without fuss.

Today he cooks for private tables, writes menus for restaurants and hotels, and caters events. He still shops for his own fish.

Beliefs

Three rules in every kitchen.

Season

Cook what is here.

The menu follows the market, not the other way round.

Fire

Heat is an ingredient.

Char, smoke and slow braise do more than any sauce.

Restraint

Leave something off.

A plate is finished when nothing more can be taken away.

Experience

Kitchens along the way.

  1. 2004

    A family taverna

    Starts at fifteen peeling vegetables and learning fire, bread and patience from the cook who raised him in the kitchen.

  2. 2008

    Fine dining, Athens

    Commis, then sous chef, in a tasting-menu kitchen. Learns precision, timing and how to run a pass.

  3. 2013

    Head chef, boutique hotel

    Leads the kitchen of a 24-room hotel on the Aegean. Builds a menu around the island's own fish, greens and cheese.

  4. 2017

    Executive chef, resort in Cyprus

    Runs a brigade of eighteen across three restaurants and banqueting for up to 300 guests.

  5. 2020

    Consulting begins

    Rewrites menus and costing for restaurants and hotels, and trains kitchen teams before openings.

  6. 2024

    Private chef

    Opens his table to private clients and event hosts across Cyprus and Greece.
