About

Gregory Neocleous

BSc, MBA. Leadership and executive coach, master trainer, HR consultant and speaker. Founder of Epimorfosis Consulting.

He has devoted his working life to one question: what makes people and organisations succeed, and how to make it happen faster. His answer is practical. Clear goals, the right habits, strong relationships, and a plan you can measure.

He speaks fluent Greek and English.

Timeline

A life between Kyrenia and the world

  1. 1966

    Born in Kyrenia

    Grows up by the harbour of the town he still calls home.

  2. 1974

    Invasion and enclavement

    At eight years old he lives through the invasion and months of enclavement. In May 1975 the family is moved to Nicosia.

  3. USA

    Studies in America

    BSc in Marketing at the University of Akron, then an MBA in HR Management and Finance at the University of New Haven. As a student leader he campaigns for Cyprus on American campuses.

  4. 1991

    Back to Cyprus

    Elected to Kyrenia Municipal Council, where he serves until 2001 and represents the town at European conferences.

  5. Career

    HR and consulting

    HR Manager at PwC Cyprus, HR Director at Karvo Companies in the USA, senior consultant with international consulting firms.

  6. Epimorfosis

    Founds his own firm

    Epimorfosis Consulting: corporate training, coaching, psychometric testing and HR consulting. As an IIP Advisor he takes a client to IIP Platinum, the highest level of Investors in People.

  7. 2020–25

    Washington DC

    Lives in the US with his family and serves at the Consular Section of the Embassy of the Republic of Cyprus.

  8. Today

    Nicosia

    Back in Cyprus, coaching, training and speaking. Lives in Nicosia with his wife Maria and their daughter Karolina.
