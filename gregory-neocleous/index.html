His story

From Kyrenia, to America, and home again.

Born in Kyrenia in 1966, Gregory was eight when the invasion turned his family into refugees. He studied in the United States, came back in 1991 to serve his town on the Kyrenia Municipal Council, and has spent his working life since helping people and companies grow.

From PwC Cyprus to an HR directorship in the USA, from his own firm Epimorfosis Consulting to the Embassy of Cyprus in Washington DC, he brings the same belief to every room: people change when they see what is possible.
